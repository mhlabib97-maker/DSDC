Rhetoric

Established in 2015, the Dhaka Shaheen Debating Club (DSDC) stands as the beacon of logic at BAF Shaheen College Dhaka. We are dedicated to forging thinkers and speakers who navigate the complexities of the modern world with eloquence.

Through high-intensity training and national tournaments, we transform students into articulate leaders. Our philosophy: Logic over noise.
